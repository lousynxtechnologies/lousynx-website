What's in the draft

  • Accounts for seniors and for helpers: sign up, log in, edit your profile, change your password
  • Big, simple screens with a larger-text button
  • Post a request in a few taps: chat, shopping, companionship, other
  • Helper view to accept requests and check in
  • Chat once matched, to agree where to meet and what to do (with quick replies and safety warnings)
  • Safety code the senior shares with the helper at the door
  • Live map: seniors and helpers see each other once matched; open requests show only a rough area
  • One-tap call to a family contact and to emergency services
  • Two-way reviews: seniors see nearby helpers' ratings, helpers see each requester's rating, and both review each other after a visit

Read before using

  • Prototype only. Accounts, requests and profiles stay on your device (passwords are stored only as salted hashes), other people on the map are simulated, and no payments exist.
  • Not an emergency, medical or personal-care service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
  • A real launch needs a backend, helper identity and background checks, privacy compliance and payments through a licensed provider.
  • Not legal advice. Rules for in-home visits and vulnerable people differ by region.
