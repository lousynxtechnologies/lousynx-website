Educational Travel Planning

International learning journeys that combine education, culture and care.

A great educational trip changes how people see the world. We design meaningful international learning experiences that tie each day to clear learning outcomes, balance structure with discovery, and put safety and care first. We also provide digital tools that keep groups informed, on schedule and connected.

The challenge

  • Itineraries often focus on sightseeing, and the learning goals get lost.
  • Group logistics, permissions, budgets and safety planning demand a lot of unpaid effort from teachers and organisers.
  • Parents and administrators want transparency, clear communication and a plan for when things go wrong.
  • Students gain more when they prepare before the trip and reflect afterwards, but few programmes support that.

Who this is for

  • Schools, colleges and universities planning study tours or exchanges
  • Language and cultural programmes
  • Corporate learning journeys and leadership exchanges
  • Families and community groups that want a guided learning trip

What we deliver

Practical building blocks, combined to fit your goals. We pick the pieces that matter for you.

Curriculum-aligned itinerary design

Each day connects places, people and activities to your learning outcomes, with a balance of guided visits, hands-on activities and free time.

Study tours and cultural exchange

Programmes for languages, history, science, arts, business and community engagement, built with local educators and hosts.

Learning materials

Pre-trip briefings, on-trip audio guides and prompts, and post-trip reflection activities that make the learning stick.

Logistics and budgeting

Transport, accommodation and activity planning with transparent costs, arranged through suitable licensed or registered partners where bookings are involved.

Safety and duty-of-care planning

Risk assessments, supervision plans, consent and emergency procedures, and spoken or written reminders at each step of the day.

Digital tools for groups

An itinerary app with reminders, maps, audio guides, emergency contacts and a simple way to share updates with families.

How we work

A clear, collaborative process with something tangible at every stage.

  1. Define

    Agree learning outcomes, group size, ages, budget, dates and any accessibility or dietary needs.

  2. Design

    Draft the itinerary and activities, with options and clear costs, for review by your team.

  3. Prepare

    Confirm partners and logistics, brief participants and families, and complete safety planning.

  4. Travel

    Run the trip with reminders, check-ins, audio guides and a responsive support contact.

  5. Reflect

    Debrief, collect feedback and produce a summary of outcomes for your institution.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle the bookings?

We design and plan the programme. Where bookings or the sale of travel services are involved, we make sure appropriately licensed or registered partners are in place, and we explain who is responsible for what.

How do you approach safety and safeguarding?

Safety planning is part of the design: risk assessments, supervision plans, consent, emergency contacts and clear procedures. Requirements differ by school, region and destination, so we align the plan with your institution's policies.

Can the programme be tailored to our curriculum?

Yes. We start from your learning outcomes and build the itinerary around them.

What about accessibility and different needs?

We ask about mobility, dietary, medical and learning needs at the start and design options accordingly. We also look at accessible transport, accommodation and activities.

How do you protect participant data, including minors?

We collect only what is needed, limit who can see it and explain how it is used. For minors we follow your institution's consent and privacy requirements.
