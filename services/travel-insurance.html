Innovative Travel Insurance

Design trip-aware, data-driven insurance experiences with the right licensed partners.

Travel insurance is often bought in a hurry, rarely understood and painful to claim on. We help insurers, brokers and travel brands design technology-driven products and journeys that are clearer to buy, easier to use during a trip and faster to claim, using data and emerging technology responsibly.

The challenge

  • Policies are long and confusing, so customers do not know what they are covered for.
  • Claims require forms, receipts and phone calls at the worst possible moment.
  • Cover is generic and rarely reflects the trip a customer is actually taking.
  • Data could improve products and service, but it must be handled with care, transparency and compliance.

Who this is for

  • Insurers, managing general agents and brokers exploring digital travel products
  • Travel brands, booking platforms and tour operators considering embedded cover
  • Education and group-travel organisers who need group-level cover and visibility
  • Start-ups building insurance-related travel features with licensed partners
Please noteLousynx Technologies provides design and technology services. Insurance products must be underwritten, distributed and advised on by appropriately licensed and regulated parties in each jurisdiction (for example, by licensed insurers and brokers, under the rules of regulators such as FSRA in Ontario). Nothing on this page is insurance advice or an offer of insurance.

What we deliver

Practical building blocks, combined to fit your goals. We pick the pieces that matter for you.

Product concepts and research

Customer interviews, journey mapping and concept testing to find the product ideas customers actually want and will understand.

Trip-aware digital journeys

Quote, purchase and in-trip experiences that use itinerary context, such as dates, destinations and activities, to show relevant information at the right moment.

Event-triggered cover concepts

Design and prototype concepts such as delay-triggered benefits, including the data feeds, rules and customer messaging they would need, with insurers and legal review.

A better claims experience

Guided digital claim capture with photos, documents and voice descriptions, AI-assisted triage and clear status updates, with people reviewing decisions.

Analytics support

Dashboards and models that help actuarial, product and service teams understand customer behaviour and claims patterns, working alongside your licensed experts.

Embedded and partner integrations

APIs and widgets for booking platforms, apps and partners, plus the assistance information travellers need, such as emergency numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Are you an insurer or a broker?

This service covers design and technology. Insurance products must be underwritten and distributed by appropriately licensed and regulated parties, and we design with them. We can help you identify and work with the right partners.

Can you launch an insurance product for us?

We can design, prototype and build the digital experience and integrations. The product itself, its wording, pricing and approvals sit with your licensed insurer, broker and legal advisors.

How do you handle sensitive data in claims?

We treat claims data, which can include health and financial information, as highly sensitive: minimal collection, strong access control, encryption and clear consent. Requirements depend on the region and the partners involved.

Is AI used to decide claims?

We use AI to assist, for example by reading documents and triaging cases, and we keep people responsible for decisions. We also test for fairness and keep audit trails.

What does the Wayly prototype have to do with insurance?

It shows the kind of trip context, such as dates, flights, hotels, transfers and emergency numbers, that could support trip-aware journeys in the future. It is not an insurance product.
