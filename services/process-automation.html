Business Process Automation

Free your team from repetitive work, with a human in the loop where it matters.

Every organisation has tasks that people repeat every day: copying data between systems, chasing approvals, sorting documents and sending reminders. We map those processes, automate the ones that are worth it, and keep people in control of exceptions, so your team spends time on judgment and relationships instead of copy-and-paste.

The challenge

  • Staff spend hours moving data between systems, and mistakes creep in.
  • Approvals stall in inboxes and nobody sees where a request is stuck.
  • Documents such as invoices, forms and bookings arrive in many formats and are typed in by hand.
  • Past automation attempts broke when rules changed because nobody could maintain them.

Who this is for

  • Operations, finance and customer-service teams drowning in manual tasks
  • Travel and hospitality businesses with booking and itinerary workflows
  • Organisations with many documents, forms and approvals
  • Teams that want automation without losing oversight

What we deliver

Practical building blocks, combined to fit your goals. We pick the pieces that matter for you.

Process discovery and mapping

We document how work really flows, measure time and error rates and identify the steps most worth automating.

Workflow automation

Approvals, routing, notifications, deadlines and escalations, with a clear view of where each item is.

System integration

Connect your tools through APIs and webhooks so data is entered once and flows everywhere it is needed.

Document processing

OCR and AI extraction for invoices, forms and bookings, with validation rules and human review of anything uncertain.

AI assistants and triage

Assistants that categorise requests, draft replies and route cases, always within guardrails you control.

Monitoring and governance

Logs, alerts, audit trails and exception queues so you can trust and maintain the automation over time.

How we work

A clear, collaborative process with something tangible at every stage.

  1. Map

    Walk through the real process with the people who do the work and capture the steps, rules and exceptions.

  2. Prioritise

    Score candidates by time saved, error reduction, risk and effort, and choose a first process.

  3. Pilot

    Automate one process end to end with human checkpoints, and measure against the baseline.

  4. Scale

    Extend to more processes using shared building blocks, with documentation and training.

  5. Monitor

    Track volume, errors and exceptions, and adjust rules as the business changes.

Technology and approach

We choose tools to fit your stack, budget and risk profile, rather than the other way around.

  • Workflow engines and low-code tools (n8n, Make, Power Automate) for lighter needs
  • Custom services with queues and schedulers for critical flows
  • API and webhook integrations
  • Python for data handling and glue code
  • OCR and document AI with validation
  • LLM-based extraction and drafting, always with review rules
  • Audit logging and alerting
  • Role-based access and approvals

Frequently asked questions

Which processes should we automate first?

Usually those that are repetitive, rule-based, high-volume and error-prone. We score candidates together and start where the payoff is clear and the risk is low.

Will automation replace our staff?

The aim is to remove repetitive work, not people. We design for a human in the loop: staff handle exceptions, approvals and the work that needs judgment.

What happens when our rules change?

We keep rules configurable and documented, and we build monitoring so changes are noticed quickly instead of causing silent failures.

How do you measure return on investment?

We capture a baseline before we start, such as time per task and error rates, and measure the same things after launch.

Is AI always needed?

No. Many processes are best served by straightforward rules and integrations. We use AI where it adds real value, such as reading messy documents or classifying requests, and keep humans in control.
