Smart Tourism Design

Digital experiences that guide visitors, delight them and spread the benefits.

Visitors expect to find their way, learn the story of a place and get help on the spot, in their own language and at their own pace. We design smart tourism experiences that do exactly that: audio-guided tours, intelligent itineraries, voice concierges and visitor insights that help destinations and businesses thrive.

The challenge

  • Visitors struggle to find information, miss lesser-known sites and crowd the same few places.
  • Printed maps and static signs cannot adapt to language, mobility or time of day.
  • Operators lack data about how visitors move, and what they enjoy, to improve offerings.
  • Content gets out of date, or is too costly to produce in several languages.

Who this is for

  • Destinations, visitor centres and tourism boards
  • Attractions, museums, heritage sites and tour operators
  • Hotels and travel brands building a digital concierge
  • Cities planning sustainable, well-distributed visitor flows

What we deliver

Practical building blocks, combined to fit your goals. We pick the pieces that matter for you.

Audio guides and scenic descriptions

Narrated tours with short stops and vivid scenic descriptions, playable on any phone, with text alongside for accessibility.

Smart itineraries and wayfinding

Itineraries that adapt to time, interests and mobility, with maps, navigation links and nearby-sight suggestions.

Voice concierge

A voice-first assistant that answers questions, reminds visitors of the next step and helps them get a taxi or directions.

Multilingual content

Translated and locally voiced content, reviewed by people who know the place, with a workflow for keeping it current.

Visitor insights

Privacy-friendly analytics on routes, dwell time and demand, to help plan staffing, opening hours and promotion.

Sustainable and inclusive design

Nudges toward off-peak times and local businesses, and experiences that work for different abilities, ages and languages.

How we work

A clear, collaborative process with something tangible at every stage.

  1. Understand

    Map visitor journeys, pain points and stakeholders with the people who know the destination.

  2. Curate

    Choose stories, sites and routes, and write or source content with local experts.

  3. Design

    Prototype the experience, voice and interface and test it with real visitors.

  4. Launch

    Release a pilot with signage, QR entry points and staff briefing.

  5. Learn

    Use feedback and anonymous usage data to refine content and extend to more sites.

Where it makes a difference

Examples of what this service looks like in practice.

Illustrative scenario

A self-guided heritage walk

Visitors scan a code, choose a route and hear short audio stops as they walk, with scenic descriptions for the views and links to navigate to the next stop. Our Wayly prototype includes this pattern.

Illustrative scenario

A voice concierge for a hotel or visitor centre

Guests ask what is on today, how to get to a sight or when the next shuttle leaves, and get spoken answers drawn from approved information.

Illustrative scenario

Spreading demand with seasonal challenges

Reward visits to lesser-known sites or off-peak times with in-kind perks. Our GuildPool prototype explores seasonal challenges and sponsor-funded rewards.

These scenarios are illustrative examples of the kinds of problems we help solve. They are not descriptions of specific client engagements, and results depend on your data, context and goals.

Technology and approach

We choose tools to fit your stack, budget and risk profile, rather than the other way around.

  • Installable PWAs with offline caching
  • Text-to-speech and neural voices, human narration where it matters
  • Maps and geofencing (OpenStreetMap, Mapbox and similar)
  • Content management for stories, routes and translations
  • Privacy-friendly analytics
  • Ticketing, booking and payment integrations via APIs
  • QR and NFC entry points
  • Accessibility features: captions, large text, high contrast

Our principles

  • Start small, show working results early
  • Privacy, security and accessibility by design
  • Keep people in control of important decisions
  • Document everything so you are never locked in

Frequently asked questions

Do the guides work without a mobile connection?

They can. Installable web apps cache content so tours keep working offline, which matters in museums, rural areas and for visitors avoiding roaming charges.

Which languages can you support?

Any language for which we can obtain accurate translations and suitable voices. We recommend professional translation and local review for important content.

How do you make sure the content is accurate?

We work with local historians, guides and site managers, cite sources internally and set up a review process. The prototype content on this site is a general-knowledge demo and should not be used for real visitors without verification.

Can this connect to our ticketing or booking systems?

Yes, through your providers' APIs. We scope integrations carefully, because they affect security, payments and support.

What about visitor privacy?

We collect as little as possible, ask before using location, and prefer anonymous, aggregated analytics. Voice and location features are optional and explained clearly to the visitor.
