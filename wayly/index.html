Wayly DRAFT

Your AI travel concierge. Plan the trip, then let Wayly walk you through it by voice: check-in reminders, a taxi when you need one, and audio-guided tours with scenic descriptions at each attraction.

Try the app Install on your phone

What's in the draft

  • Plan: search sample flights and hotels, pick an airport transfer, and get a priced summary
  • Trip: a step-by-step itinerary (check-in opens, leave for the airport, boarding, landing, hotel check-in, tours, check-out) with spoken reminders
  • Taxi help: a ride card with a "show to the driver" screen, an Uber link and map directions
  • Guide: audio tours and "describe the view" scenic descriptions for 15 sights in Toronto, Paris, Tokyo, New York and London, plus nearby sights using your location
  • Concierge: ask by typing or by voice: "What's next?", "Call a taxi", "Tell me about the Eiffel Tower"
  • Demo trip: fast-forward through a whole trip and hear every reminder in minutes

Read before using

  • Prototype only. Flights, hotels, prices and bookings are sample data and are simulated: nothing is reserved and no payment is taken.
  • The concierge is a simple rule-based demo, not a live AI. A full version needs a server with an AI language model and live travel data.
  • Reminders speak and show only while the app is open. Reminders when the app is closed need push notifications from a server.
  • Landmark notes are short general summaries. Always check official sources for opening hours, tickets, visas and travel rules.
  • Your trip stays on this device. Location is used only when you ask for nearby sights. Voice input (the microphone button) uses your browser's speech service.
