Install tips

🤖 Android

  1. Tap Download for Android, then open GuildPool.apk.
  2. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.

Not on Google Play yet, so Play Protect may show a warning. Android 6.0 or newer.

🍎 iPhone & iPad

  1. In Safari, tap Download for iPhone, then Allow.
  2. Open Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install.

iOS shows “Not Verified” because this draft isn't from the App Store. The profile only adds a GuildPool icon to your Home Screen; remove it in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Prefer not to? In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

What's in the draft

  • Playtime and achievement logging → reputation points
  • Monthly sponsor “seasons” (e.g. 50h of FPS → share a prize pool)
  • Play-with-me: time-rich players help time-poor players (co-op only)
  • Sponsor in-kind reward redemption

Read before using

  • Prototype only: data stays on your device; sponsors and prizes are placeholders.
  • Points have no cash value and can't be bought, sold or withdrawn.
  • Game-account connectors (Steam, Riot, PSN, Nintendo) are not built yet. They'd be opt-in and use official APIs, not Accessibility Service.
  • Not legal advice. Prize pools and rewards can fall under gambling, money-transmission, consumer and tax rules depending on where you operate. Get legal review before a real launch.
